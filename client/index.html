How to Play Table Tennis

Control mechanics and official table tennis rules.

🎯 Paddle Movement: Move mouse or drag finger across baseline. Racket smoothly mirrors your movement with zero latency.

🏓 Automatic Clean Contact: Position your racket in the ball's bounce path. Contact triggers an authentic baseline-to-baseline return over the net.

⚡ Swing Speed & Spin: Swipe or flick through the ball quickly in your desired direction to add smash speed, topspin, and sharp angles.

🏆 Official Match Rules: Ball must bounce once on your half before you hit it. First player to 11 points (lead by 2 points) wins the set!
