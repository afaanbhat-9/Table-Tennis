How to Play
Intuitive controls & table tennis rules

🎯 Racket Movement: Move your mouse or drag your touch finger. The racket follows your position smoothly and accurately across the table baseline.

🏓 Automatic Contact: Simply align the racket with the incoming ball after it bounces on your side. The racket automatically strikes a clean return over the net!

⚡ Power & Placement: Swipe or flick in your desired stroke direction to add topspin, smash speed, or sharp cross-court angles.

🏆 Official ITTF Rules: The ball must bounce once on your half before you hit it. First player to 11 points (win by 2 margin) takes the victory!
